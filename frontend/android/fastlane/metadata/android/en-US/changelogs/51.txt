Pre-F-Droid audit release. Highlights:
- Signup fixed: typing into the captcha no longer closes the dialog.
- Your data, your rules: download the whole account as one JSON file or
  delete the account yourself (Settings -> Data & account). New privacy
  page linked from the footer.
- Opening the app offline no longer logs you out.
- Server switcher now available in Settings on every Android build.
- Android back button closes dialogs instead of exiting the app.
- Review: keyboard shortcuts (space reveals, 1-4 grade); grades are
  logged under the modality you actually practiced.
- 苹果 is "apple" again, not the tech company - proper-noun dictionary
  readings no longer win collisions.
- Smaller APK (source maps removed), assorted hardening server-side.
