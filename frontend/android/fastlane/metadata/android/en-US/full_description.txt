Read any Chinese text against the HSK 1–9 vocabulary list and study what you don't know yet.

Features
• Paste, import a URL, drop an EPUB / PDF, or scan a page with the camera
• Every word colour-coded by HSK level or by your personal progress
• Click for definitions sourced from CC-CEDICT; click sentences for translation
• Audio narration for words and full passages, with speed control
• Spaced-repetition review (FSRS) with four modes: Recognition, Dictation, Writing, Cloze
• Pronunciation check with per-syllable tone scoring (faster-whisper + librosa)
• Personal glossaries that override the dictionary for specialised corpora
• Simplified ⇄ Traditional toggle across the whole app
• Export vocabulary to Anki or CSV
• Local daily reminders, streak tracking, no trackers, no ads

Open source under the MIT licence. The Android app is a thin Capacitor wrapper around the web app; an internet connection is required because the analysis, dictionary lookup, and review scheduling live on the server.

By default the app talks to qingdu.itvoodoo.at (a free public demo run by the maintainer). You can point it at any self-hosted Qingdu backend via Settings → Server. Both the server and the app are MIT-licenced — see github.com/mkampl/qingdu.

Anti-Features: NonFreeNet — the app depends on a server, but the server source is in the same repository under the MIT licence and you can self-host it.
